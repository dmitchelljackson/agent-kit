title: An atom is the unit of knowledge
---

An atom is one markdown file holding one piece of knowledge that could be referenced, changed,
or reused on its own: one rule, one decision, one BDD case, one API endpoint, one tool, one setup step.
Everything substantive in the wiki lives in atoms; pages only arrange them.
Why: a fact that lives in exactly one place is updated once and stays correct everywhere it's embedded.

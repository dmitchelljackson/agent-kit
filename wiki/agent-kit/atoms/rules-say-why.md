title: Rules say why
---

Every rule atom ends with a `Why:` line: the failure it prevents or the reason behind it.
Why: the reason is what lets an agent apply a rule sensibly in cases the rule didn't anticipate,
and it's what the share judge uses to decide whether a rule is general.

title: Slice as finely as the meaning allows
---

Split content into the smallest pieces that still make sense alone. A page with one atom is fine.
Don't split where order *is* the meaning (a step-by-step flow, a narrative argument): that stays one atom.
If you find yourself writing "see above" inside an atom, it probably belongs with its neighbor.
Why: small atoms can be shared, synced, judged, and optimized independently.

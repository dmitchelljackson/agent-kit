title: "Don't repeat yourself"
---

Every fact lives in exactly one atom. When another page needs it, embed the atom (`![[name]]`) or link
it; never copy or restate it. If you notice two atoms saying the same thing, merge them (keep one,
`akit atom rm` the other, re-point its embeds) and log it.
Why: copies drift, and a wiki that contradicts itself is worse than no wiki.

title: Ingest
---

To ingest a source: put it (or a pointer) in `raw/`; read it; discuss the key takeaways with the owner;
then update every affected atom, create new atoms and pages where needed, note contradictions with
existing atoms, and log it (`akit log ingest "<source>"`). One source may touch many atoms.

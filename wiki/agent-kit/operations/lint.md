title: Lint
---

When asked, or when the wiki feels messy: run `akit check`, then look for contradictions, stale claims,
duplicate atoms that should merge, oversized atoms that should split, pages whose summaries lie, and
important concepts with no atom. Also flag bookmarks whose pages are never read. Fix what's safe;
ask about the rest; log it (`akit log lint "<scope>"`).

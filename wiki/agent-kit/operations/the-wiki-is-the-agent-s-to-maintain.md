title: "The wiki is the agent's to maintain"
---

Humans curate sources, direct the work, and make decisions. Agents do the bookkeeping: writing and
updating atoms, keeping pages and the index current, cross-referencing, and flagging contradictions.
When your work locks a decision, changes behavior, or teaches something future work needs, update
the wiki in the same change. Pattern: Karpathy's LLM Wiki, sliced into atoms.

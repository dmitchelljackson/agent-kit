title: Index and home page
---

`wiki/_page.md` is the home page (written by humans and agents). `wiki/index.md` is the catalog of
every page with its one-line summary, generated by `akit index` (akit also refreshes it after
structural changes). Read the index first to find relevant pages, then read only those.
Keep page summaries accurate: they are how agents decide what to open.

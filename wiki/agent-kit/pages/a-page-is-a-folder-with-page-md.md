title: A page is a folder with _page.md
---

A page is a folder containing `_page.md`, its atoms, any supporting files (scripts, configs, images),
and optionally sub-pages (sub-folders with their own `_page.md`). Pages nest like a Notion workspace:
rules, reference, decisions, and tools are all just pages.

`_page.md` has frontmatter (`title`, `summary`, optional `bookmark`) and a body that holds only
structure: headings, a sentence or two of connective context, and embeds.

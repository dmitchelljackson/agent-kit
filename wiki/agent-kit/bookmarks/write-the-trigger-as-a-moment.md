title: Write the trigger as a moment
---

The trigger starts with "Use" and names an action: "Use before writing or editing Kotlin unit tests",
"Use when adding a Room migration". Not a topic ("Android stuff") and not a rule.
Agents match on this text alone, so be specific about the action and the scope.

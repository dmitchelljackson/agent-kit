title: Number cases and never reuse numbers
---

The feature number is the page key (F-03 → feature 3). Case atoms are titled `<feature>.<case> Short name` (e.g. `3.4 Refresh failure keeps cached list`). Never renumber or reuse a number; retire a case with `akit atom rm` and log it.
